Everything You Need to Know Before Booking

From booking process to vehicle options, here's everything you need to know before booking with Tourixa Travel.

Get in touch

Get in touch

What tour packages and taxi services do you offer?

We provide customized Odisha tour packages (Puri, Konark, Chilika, Bhubaneswar), domestic tours across India, airport transfers, and car rentals (Sedan, SUV, Tempo Traveller, Luxury).

Can I customize my tour package and vehicle?

Yes! Tour duration, destinations, hotel category, and vehicle options (AC Dzire, Innova Crysta, 13–25 seater Tempo Travellers) can be fully customized.

Do you provide vehicles with experienced drivers?

Yes, all vehicles come with professional, background-verified, punctual drivers with extensive route knowledge.

What is included in the tour and taxi packages?

Packages include comfortable AC transportation, fuel, toll charges, parking, driver allowance, sightseeing pickup/drop, and 24×7 customer assistance.

How do I book a tour or taxi with Tourixa?

You can book instantly via WhatsApp (+91 9692 66 5166) or phone (+91 9692 69 5166). Our team assists you 24×7.
